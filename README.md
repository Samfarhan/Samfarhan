## 💫 About Me

```yaml
Name: Farhan Khan
Role: Founder & Lead Architect
Flagship: Nyayi AI (formerly Nyaya-Setu)
Education: Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)
Core Mission: "Democratizing Indian justice by making legal aid accessible to every citizen"
Current Focus: Production-Grade AI Systems, Agentic Workflows & Cloud Scalability
```

- 🔭 **Currently Building:** **[Nyayi AI (न्यायी)](https://ai.nyayi.in)** — India's premier AI legal intelligence assistant empowering ordinary citizens with BNS/BNSS 2023 statutory guidance, FIR filing wizards, and evidence review.
- 🏛️ **Public Knowledge Hub:** **[Nyayi.in](https://nyayi.in)** — Comprehensive Indian legal literacy portal with searchable statutory dictionaries, rights manuals, and consumer guides.
- 🌱 **Constantly Learning:** Advanced full-stack engineering, high-concurrency Node.js/Python architectures, and cutting-edge autonomous AI coding partners.
- 👯 **Open to Collaboration:** Ambitious AI + Legal-Tech innovations, high-impact civic-tech tools, and open-source projects solving real human problems.
- 💬 **Ask Me About:** Indian legal frameworks (BNS, BNSS, BSA, IPC), full-stack web architecture, Progressive Web Apps (PWA), or building scalable apps with AI partners.
- ⚡ **Fun Fact:** *"Nyaya-Setu"* translates to *"Bridge of Justice"* — bridging the divide between statutory legal codes and 1.4 billion citizens.
